# Rizal Plaza: rebuild to the 2026 design

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Revised and implemented. Not committed.

## Why

The plaza was renovated, and the model shows the old design. The project owner asked to use the 2026 version, including the west side, where the model had a building that doesn't exist.

## What the imagery shows

Street View at the same spot on Barreto St:

| Date | Plaza |
|---|---|
| 2022 (original tour photos) | Cream panels, terracotta and grey rings, tiered blue monument base, blue benches and bins, dark metal arches |
| February 2023 | Same design |
| August 2024 | Same design |
| **January 2026** | **Renovated:** see below |

So the renovation happened between August 2024 and January 2026. Street View can't give a closer date. Other imagery of the plaza, including the satellite view, may be older.

## Position

The model's plaza is too far east and too wide, and doesn't reach S.V. Rizal St. OpenStreetMap's park outline and monument point, plus the views above, put its centre line about 24 m further west.

| | Model before | Now |
|---|---:|---:|
| Centre line | x 34 | x 10 |
| Width | 32 m | 18 m |
| Depth from Barreto St | 67 m | About 41 m to the arch |

The plaza no longer lines up with the Old Building's portico. That matches the street views: the portico is east of the plaza's centre line.

## Known limits

- **The 360° photos stay old.** Flagpole and Monument still open the 2022 photos, so they won't match the new 3D plaza until someone reshoots them.
- **Sizes are estimates** from street photos, OpenStreetMap, and the scale bar, so they need checking on site.
- **Google is a visual reference only.** No Google images are stored. Positions lean on OpenStreetMap, but the design details were read from Street View, so check with your adviser about academic use.

## Outcome

- **Built as planned,** and checked in the app from above, from Barreto St, and along S.V. Rizal St, with no console errors.
- **Results:** type checking is clean. Vitest passed 101 of 101. Playwright passed 26 of 26 in the regression, seats, smoke, and `voice.spec.ts` files. `voice-two-way.spec.ts` wasn't run, because voice code didn't change.
- **Tests changed to match the move:** the middle of the plaza in `seats.test.ts` and the plaza surface point in `physics.test.ts`.
- **Simplified:** shops have plain coloured signs with no words, the church is one plain block, and the market is three small stalls, a cart, and one umbrella.
- **Not checked:** real phones, and sizes on site.

## Revision — 2026-11-03: original position, two-lane market road

The project owner reviewed the moved plaza and asked for two corrections:

- **Put the plaza back in its original position.** It now covers x 18 to 50 and z −125 to −58 again, centred at x 34 again. The 2026 design stays. This replaces the Position section above. OpenStreetMap still places the real plaza further west, so this is a deliberate choice and not a data match.
- **Make the market road two lanes.** The stretch beside the plaza is now 7 m wide, down from 10 m.

To keep the road beside the plaza, as the owner's photos show, the road and market moved with it. The road now runs at x 12.5, and it joins Barreto St there. S.V. Rizal St south of Barreto St doesn't change, so traffic follows a short stretch of Barreto St between the two parts. The real bend is smaller than this.

The flagpole stays behind the arch as in the 2026 photos, so it moved from z −105 to z −121.5. Burgos St and the church moved north, past the plaza's restored north end.

**Second check against the owner's January 2026 photo from Barreto St:**

- **Width:** the 32 m width fits the photo better than the 18 m version, which looked too small.
- **Monument:** the statue stands well back, closer to the arch than to the entrance, so it moved to z −98. Its depth is judged from the photo's perspective.
- **Front of the plaza:** the first part is open grey paving, so the pergolas and benches now start at about z −76.
